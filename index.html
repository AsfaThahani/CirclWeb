Four ways to Circl

Whatever you need, someone nearby has it

Every request on Circl falls into one of four categories. Pick what fits, post it, and let the neighbourhood respond.

Borrow

Borrow

Lend a hand, lend a drill. Borrow what you need for a day, return it, and you're done. Completely free with an optional deposit if needed.

e.g. Pressure Washer: Karcher K2
Rent

Rent

Need gear for a shoot, a trip, or a weekend project? Rent it by the day from someone just two streets over.

e.g. DSLR Camera: Nikon D3500 (Rs 1,200/day)
Favour

Favour

Free help freely given. Moving a sofa, walking a dog, or watering plants while your neighbours are away.

e.g. Help moving a sofa (2 people)
Task

Paid Task

Turn a free afternoon into extra income by offering tutoring, photography, deliveries, or other jobs.

e.g. A/L Maths Tutoring

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your local circle with Circl.

Yes. Safety is our primary concern. All members are verified using secure NIC checks. Proximity is computed as general distances (e.g. 0.4 km away), and your precise location is never shared on the public map to guarantee your privacy.

When posting an item for Rent, you can choose a security deposit (e.g., LKR 1000/5000/10000). The borrower authorizes this deposit before receiving the item. If the item is returned damaged or lost, a dispute can be raised immediately to protect your hardware value.

For rental requests and paid tasks, payments are processed seamlessly inside the app. Users can top up their in-app Wallet using debit/credit cards or transfer funds directly to local bank accounts.

You start at Bronze Aura. By completing transactions, lending items, helping on favor requests, and receiving 5-star ratings, you gain reputation points. Reaching 300 points promotes you to Silver, 500 to Gold, and 1000 to Diamond, unlocking perks like lower platform fees and higher visibility.
